The minimum payment is the smallest amount a card holder must pay in a billing cycle to stay in good standing, meaning to avoid late fees, default and collection steps.

The issuer sets this amount and shows it on the monthly statement (estado de cuenta). It can change from one cycle to the next, so read it each time instead of assuming it stays the same.

Paying the minimum keeps the account current, but the rest of the balance keeps earning interest. If your payment covers everything that would otherwise carry interest, no interest is charged. If it does not, the unpaid part grows with the interest added to it, and clearing the debt can take a long time.

Paying only the minimum can make a small balance last for years. Paying more than the minimum reduces the balance faster and cuts the total interest. Compare the total cost of each option, not only the monthly figure.

This is general information, not a recommendation.
