The minimum payment is the smallest amount a card holder must pay in a billing cycle to stay in good standing, meaning to avoid late fees, default and collection steps.

The issuer sets this amount and shows it on the monthly statement (estado de cuenta). Because it can vary from one cycle to the next, it is worth checking on each statement.

Paying the minimum keeps the account current, but the rest of the balance keeps earning interest. A payment that clears the whole balance for the cycle avoids interest for that period; a smaller one leaves a part that is added to the debt along with its own interest, and paying it off can take a long time.

With payments that only barely reach the minimum, a small debt can drag on for years. Paying above the minimum shortens the timeline and lowers the total interest you pay. What is worth comparing is the total cost of each option, not only the monthly figure.

This is general information, not a recommendation.
