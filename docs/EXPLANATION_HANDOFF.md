# Financial explanation handoff to Role B (FC-5)

**Delivered catalog, pending integration decisions.** `contracts/catalog/financial_core.catalog.json` lists existing financial `Fact` key templates, warning codes and error-field patterns; `backend/tests/contract/test_fact_catalog.py` checks static source sites and runtime responses. A warning is not the same thing as a fact: facts carry a key, value, unit and derivation; warnings carry code, message and optional `must_mention` tokens. Do not infer a number from model prose.

- `RECURRING_PROVISIONAL` means a two-payment replacement for an ended recurring item is treated as a commitment at the last amount, not that the pattern is certain. `CATEGORY_SPARSE` means categories with fewer than six observed months have been combined into `other` (zeros included); do not present `other` as a merchant or a precise original category. In ledger mode both reach tool envelopes under ADR-017 (Proposed; see below).
- `SEASONALITY_UNOBSERVED` (ADR-016, Proposed) means at least one configured seasonal month (fc-0.1: December) has no pay cycle in the estimation window, so no seasonal uplift was applied for it (factor 1) and that month's spend may be under-forecast. It does not mean the month will be normal. It is one warning listing every missing month in ascending order; `must_mention` holds the English month names plus `seasonal`, and the message has no ledger text. The paired fact `estimate.seasonal.unobserved_months` is a string of ascending month numbers (e.g. `"12"` or `"7,12"`, unit `month_numbers`), not an amount. Under ADR-017 (Proposed) it reaches ledger-mode tool envelopes (`reaches_tool_envelope: true`) and the verifier enforces it like any other warning.
- `RATE_ASSUMED` means the offered monthly interest rate was missing and the deterministic default was used. `FIRST_INSTALLMENT_ASSUMED` identifies the chosen default month. `PROFILE_CAPPED` records an attempted profile above permitted capacity/tolerance. These tool warnings have nonempty `must_mention`; explanations must mention each emitted warning.
- `INVALID_INPUT.field` names the offending field, including indexed ledger paths such as `transactions[12].kind`; `allowed` may carry enumerated choices. `INSUFFICIENT_DATA.missing` lists missing model inputs or history; neither status authorizes presenting a computed amount. Display the actual envelope field/missing list, not a guessed fix.
- In `application/use_cases/chat.py`, verification sees the **union of facts and caveats from tools called in that turn**. Keys can collide, e.g. `liquidity_floor` appears in forecast, purchase and surplus, and `plan.amount` appears in surplus and plan. Preserve tool/snapshot context when displaying facts; do not assume one key maps to one globally unique observation across a turn.

**Forwarding proposal pending B approval (ADR-017, Proposed).** With `NL_MODEL_SOURCE=ledger`, every OK financial tool envelope now ends with the estimator's `estimate.*` facts and its warnings, after the tool's own. The fixture source (the default) forwards nothing. Non-OK envelopes carry no evidence.

- All nine estimation warnings avoid merchant, category, label and txn_id text. The five warnings with unbounded counts use static count-free wording; ADR-016's bounded seasonal-month count is unchanged. Their `must_mention` tokens are substrings of their own message. The catalog marks them `must_mention: true` and `reaches_tool_envelope: true`.
- Forwarded facts keep key, value and unit. Their derivation ends with `; source=ledger-estimator; estimator_snapshot=<id>; tool_snapshot=<id>`. A two-tool turn has two copies of each `estimate.*` key with different `tool_snapshot`; keep both and show the tool context.
- A two-tool turn also has each estimator warning twice. One mention satisfies the verifier for both copies. The deterministic narrator repeats the sentence once per tool; this is proposed shape semantics pending B approval, not deduplicated.
- An explanation that omits a forwarded warning fails with `MISSING_WARNING` and falls back to deterministic narration (`tests/application/test_warning_forwarding.py`).

B answers three yes/no questions (shape, exact wording and tokens, verifier obligation) in `docs/proposals/WARNING_FORWARDING.md` before the model-source default changes.
