# Financial explanation handoff to Role B (FC-5)

**Delivered catalog, pending integration decisions.** `contracts/catalog/financial_core.catalog.json` lists existing financial `Fact` key templates, warning codes and error-field patterns; `backend/tests/contract/test_fact_catalog.py` checks static source sites and runtime responses. A warning is not the same thing as a fact: facts carry a key, value, unit and derivation; warnings carry code, message and optional `must_mention` tokens. Do not infer a number from model prose.

- `RECURRING_PROVISIONAL` means a two-payment replacement for an ended recurring item is treated as a commitment at the last amount, not that the pattern is certain. `CATEGORY_SPARSE` means categories with fewer than six observed months have been combined into `other` (zeros included); do not present `other` as a merchant or a precise original category. Both are **estimator-only** warnings today.
- `SEASONALITY_UNOBSERVED` (ADR-016, Proposed) means at least one configured seasonal month (fc-0.1: December) has no pay cycle in the estimation window, so no seasonal uplift was applied for it (factor 1) and that month's spend may be under-forecast. It does not mean the month will be normal. It is one warning listing every missing month in ascending order; `must_mention` holds the English month names plus `seasonal`, and the message has no ledger text. The paired fact `estimate.seasonal.unobserved_months` is a string of ascending month numbers (e.g. `"12"` or `"7,12"`, unit `month_numbers`), not an amount. It is the only estimation caveat with non-empty `must_mention` (catalog `must_mention: true`), but it is still **estimator-only** (`reaches_tool_envelope: false`), so no verifier obligation applies until forwarding is decided.
- `RATE_ASSUMED` means the offered monthly interest rate was missing and the deterministic default was used. `FIRST_INSTALLMENT_ASSUMED` identifies the chosen default month. `PROFILE_CAPPED` records an attempted profile above permitted capacity/tolerance. These tool warnings have nonempty `must_mention`; explanations must mention each emitted warning.
- `INVALID_INPUT.field` names the offending field, including indexed ledger paths such as `transactions[12].kind`; `allowed` may carry enumerated choices. `INSUFFICIENT_DATA.missing` lists missing model inputs or history; neither status authorizes presenting a computed amount. Display the actual envelope field/missing list, not a guessed fix.
- In `application/use_cases/chat.py`, verification sees the **union of facts and caveats from tools called in that turn**. Keys can collide, e.g. `liquidity_floor` appears in forecast, purchase and surplus, and `plan.amount` appears in surplus and plan. Preserve tool/snapshot context when displaying facts; do not assume one key maps to one globally unique observation across a turn.

**Gap pending B decision:** `LedgerModelSource.load` returns only `FinancialModel` and discards the estimation `Envelope`'s facts and warnings. The nine estimation codes in the catalog therefore do not reach financial tool envelopes or the chat verifier; eight originate from `_caveat` with empty `must_mention`, and `SEASONALITY_UNOBSERVED` carries tokens that are discarded with it. The catalog marks those codes `reaches_tool_envelope=false`; this is descriptive, not approval to suppress warnings. Decide with B how to forward/word them and impose verifier obligations before changing the model-source default. Do not claim that the current verifier covers warnings it never receives.
