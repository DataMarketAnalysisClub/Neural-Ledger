# Synthetic backtest (FC-3)

**Delivered implementation validation, not predictive accuracy.** Run `make backtest PY=/absolute/path/to/.venv/bin/python`; the source and full deterministic output are `backend/tests/backtest/run.py` and `evidence/backtest/synthetic_backtest.json`. These are synthetic independent lognormal category totals and freelance income, rounded to 100 CLP, with a 49/30 December multiplier. The known parameters are properties of the generator, not observations about people.

The generator covers 21 pay cycles, Apr 2025 through Dec 2026. Each origin estimates only transactions dated on or before its pay-cycle end; account balances and as_of are recomputed from that prefix. The later realized draws are read separately only after estimation. Grid A uses origins after 6, 7, 8 and 9 cycles, horizon 3. December 2025 is evaluated from 6/7/8 without a previous December; the estimator uses factor 1. Grid B is a separately labelled origin after 18 cycles, with December 2025 in the trailing 12-cycle window, and evaluates Oct-Dec 2026. A 6-9-cycle history and three-month horizon cannot simultaneously contain one December and evaluate the next December, which is why Grid B exists.

The run uses 100 salaried seeds 150000-150099 and 100 freelance seeds 250000-250099, five origins per seed and source (1,000 estimates). Non-December category P80 coverage compares one realized category-cycle with the fitted P80; December conservative coverage compares the realized category sum with `SpendModel.total(month, "p80")` and has no nominal 80% claim. Freelance P20 coverage checks realized income >= fitted conservative income. Signed relative P50 error is `(estimate - realized) / realized`, not deviation from the generator median.

These zeros are observed diagnostics, not guarantees that the recurring detector cannot misclassify a different draw.

Wilson intervals are reported **only for Bernoulli coverage**, not for continuous relative errors or recovery (which have n, median, p10/p90 and mean absolute value). Origins, categories and cycles overlap within a seed: treating their hits as independent makes the Wilson intervals optimistic. The low Grid A December coverage is an adverse observed result of having no previous December; P80 coverage also falls below nominal. Do not retune the generator or estimator to force 80%, and do not extrapolate these synthetic statistics to real forecasts. The resulting JSON is sorted, timestamp-free and byte-identical across reruns with the same seeds.
